Privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

The short version

  • PLAI shows you the songs your friends love. To do that it reads the songs you like in the music apps you connect.
  • Your profile and liked songs are visible to other PLAI users. You can hide any song.
  • PLAI only reads from your music apps. It never posts, deletes or changes anything there.
  • No ads, no selling your data, no third-party analytics.
  • You can delete your account and all your data at any time in Settings.

Who runs PLAI

PLAI (plai.delaneyprojects.xyz) is run by André Delaney. Contact: andre@delaneyprojects.xyz.

What PLAI collects

When you sign up

  • From the service you sign in with (Spotify or Google): your name, email address and profile picture.
  • What you choose in PLAI: a username, a display name, a profile photo you upload, a status and profile colour.
  • A PIN, if you set one. It's stored by our sign-in provider in a protected (hashed) form; PLAI can't read it.

From the music apps you connect

  • Spotify: your Liked Songs, and a public playlist you choose to connect, plus basic account details.
  • Apple Music: songs in your library and songs you've loved.
  • Tidal: your favourite tracks and your name.
  • YouTube (YouTube Music): your channel name, the videos you've liked, and details of those videos (title, artist, artwork) so PLAI can show the music ones as songs. Read-only access.
  • Access keys (tokens) that let PLAI read the above on your behalf. They're stored securely and never shown to other people.

When you use PLAI

  • Who you follow, reactions, songs you save, songs you send and receive, replies, your song of the week, and invites.
  • Songs and profiles you open, and how often your share links are opened, to show you stats and improve recommendations.
  • When you last opened PLAI, and whether your music apps synced successfully.
  • If you turn on notifications: a notification address for your device and the device type (for example "iPhone").

How PLAI uses it

  • To show your liked songs to the friends who follow you, and their songs to you.
  • To calculate taste matches, your Friday recap, trending songs and new releases from artists you listen to.
  • To send the notifications you've turned on.
  • To keep your music apps syncing, and to tell you when a connection has expired.
  • To keep PLAI working and safe (for example, limits on how many songs can be sent per hour).

PLAI doesn't sell your data, doesn't show ads and doesn't use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Other PLAI users can see your username, display name, photo, status, the songs you like (except ones you hide), your song of the week, and who you follow.
  • Only you can see your email, PIN, music-app access keys, the songs you've hidden and your notification settings.
  • Only you and the other person can see songs sent between you, their messages and the replies.

Google user data (YouTube)

PLAI uses YouTube API Services. If you connect YouTube Music, PLAI requests read-only access to your YouTube account (the youtube.readonly permission) and uses it only to read your liked videos and channel name, to show the music among them as liked songs in PLAI.

PLAI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. PLAI doesn't use Google user data for advertising, doesn't sell it, doesn't let humans read it except with your permission or where needed for security or by law, and doesn't use it to train AI models.

By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove PLAI's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Services PLAI relies on

  • Supabase stores PLAI's database, sign-ins and photos.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Spotify, Apple, Tidal and Google (YouTube) provide your music data when you connect them.
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla deliver notifications to your device if you turn them on.
  • Song catalogues (Apple Music, MusicBrainz, Deezer, Spotify) are looked up with song names to show details like release date, credits and artwork. No personal information is sent.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept while you have a PLAI account. Sync logs are kept for 14 days and weekly recaps for a year. When you delete your account, your data is deleted straight away (backups held by our hosting provider expire within their normal backup period).

Your choices

  • Hide songs from your profile at any time.
  • Disconnect a music app in Settings, or remove PLAI's access from the app itself (Spotify, Google).
  • Turn notifications off in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings → delete my account. Or email andre@delaneyprojects.xyz and it'll be done for you.
  • Get a copy of your data, or ask a question, by emailing the same address.

Children

PLAI isn't meant for children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has made an account, email us and it'll be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will be announced in the app.
