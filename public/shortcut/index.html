Share with PLAI

Turn any song you're listening to into one PLAI link that opens on Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal or YouTube Music, straight from your music app's share button.

Add to iPhone

This shortcut is for iPhone and iPad. On Android, add PLAI to your home screen and it appears in your share menu automatically.

Install

  1. Tap Add to iPhone, then Download if Safari asks.
  2. Open the download (the ↓ icon in Safari's address bar). It opens in the Shortcuts app.
  3. Tap Add Shortcut.

Use it

  1. In Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal or YouTube Music, tap Share (then More if you see the app's own menu).
  2. Choose Share with PLAI. The first time, tap Always Allow.
  3. Pick Send… to message the link, or Copy link to paste it anywhere.

Tip: in the share sheet, tap Edit Actions and add it to Favourites so it's always at the top.
